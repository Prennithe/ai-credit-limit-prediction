5. INTERPRETATION NOTE

---

SHAP values explain how the trained machine-learning model uses
different features to arrive at its predictions. They describe
model behavior and should not be interpreted as proof of direct
causal relationships.

The dataset was originally created for credit-card default
prediction rather than specifically for credit-limit prediction.
Therefore, this project predicts a credit limit based on patterns
learned from historical customer profiles and assigned credit
limits. The output should be treated as an ML-based prediction or
recommendation rather than a guaranteed optimal banking decision.
